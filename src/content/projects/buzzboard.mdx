## What&#8217;s on, near you, that fits

<p class="zp-lede">BuzzBoard is campus event discovery for Georgia Tech, built with GT Web Dev Club between January and May 2026. It&#8217;s live.</p>

<p class="zp-big"><span class="ink" data-ink="500+">500+</span></p>

<p class="zp-cap">events on the live site.</p>

<ul>
  <li>RSVPs, friends and presence update in real time, with optimistic UI so nothing waits on the network.</li>
  <li>The feed ranks events by how close they are.</li>
  <li>Going to something that clashes with your schedule gets flagged.</li>
  <li>Events sit on a Leaflet map, and calendars come in through ICS import.</li>
</ul>

Next.js and TypeScript in front; Express, Firebase and Firestore behind.

## Near you first

Drag the pink pin, tap the map, or focus the pin and use the arrow keys. The feed on the facing page re-ranks by walking distance as you go; the numbers on the pins follow.

## The feed

RSVP and the count changes at once. The server confirms a moment later; if the save fails, the change rolls back and says so. Going to something also puts it on your day, overleaf.
